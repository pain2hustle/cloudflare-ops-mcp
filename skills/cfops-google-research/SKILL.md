# Google Source Research

## Workflow

1. Convert the question into a small query set covering the main claim, counterclaim, names, dates, and primary records.
2. Search through an available Google-compatible search/browser connector. Respect access controls, robots rules, and rate limits.
3. Prefer official documentation, original records, papers, court/government sources, and first-party statements over aggregators.
4. Record title, canonical URL, publisher, publication/update date, accessed date, and the exact claim each source supports.
5. Open the sources; do not treat snippets as evidence.
6. Run a secondary dive for missing links, contrary evidence, and newer corrections.
7. Separate verified facts, source claims, inference, uncertainty, and unsupported material.

Do not invent citations, bypass paywalls/access controls, or imply that Google ranking proves truth. Keep quotations short and comply with source-use limits.
